I am a PhD student in the [Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering](https://ise.ufl.edu/) at the [University of Florida](https://www.ufl.edu/). 
My work focuses on developing methods for solving problems in network optimization and interdiction. My research interests also include using integer programming and
data analytics to improve decision making. I enjoy working on optimization problems in areas such as infrastucture planning, network resilience, and sports analytics.
